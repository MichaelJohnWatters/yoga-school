Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This policy explains how [STUDIO NAME] ("we", the data controller) handles your personal data when you use our booking app. We process data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; the supervisory authority is the ICO.

What we collect

  • Account — your name and email address (used to sign you in and identify you to the studio).
  • Bookings & attendance — the classes you book, attend, or are waitlisted for.
  • Payments — purchases, passes, and memberships. Card details are handled directly by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Messages — class chat and any notes you send.
  • Technical — a sign-in token stored on your device, and server logs needed to run and secure the service.

Why (lawful basis)

  • Contract — to provide bookings, passes, and memberships you buy.
  • Legal obligation — to keep financial records (HMRC).
  • Legitimate interests — to run classes, prevent abuse, and keep an audit trail.

How long we keep it

Summarised from our retention register:

  • Payments / purchases — retained 6 years (HMRC); free-text notes scrubbed on erasure.
  • Bookings — retained as class history; companion ("plus-one") names scrubbed on erasure.
  • Chat / messages — kept while your account is active; bodies scrubbed on erasure.
  • Audit log — action, actor, and timestamp retained for accountability; personal detail redacted.
  • Notifications, device tokens, preferences — transient; deleted.

On erasure we pseudonymise your records rather than delete financial/audit rows we are legally required to keep. Backups rotate out within 30 days.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, file storage, and push notifications.
  • Our hosting provider — to run the service.

We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can request access, a copy (portability), correction, or erasure of your data. Ask the studio; we respond within one calendar month. You may also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
